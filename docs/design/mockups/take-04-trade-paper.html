Meridian Sets ‘Thunder County’ For November Start

Rourke to helm £20.2 million actioner; Vance toplines, but the second lead is still open with cameras nine weeks out.

By the Production Desk

Meridian Pictures has dated its fall actioner Thunder County for a 2 November start, with a 46-day board and principal photography split between the valley locations and the studio's own stages.

Dale Rourke helms from an original by Junius Bell. Rourke took the assignment after a three-way bake-off, pitching a lean, grounded read against the script's more spectacular leanings — a bold call that widens the picture's range in both directions.

Marguerite Vance toplines as the sheriff, playing the part weary rather than heroic. Halvard Nyquist lenses. Estelle Marchetti produces.

The second lead remains uncast. Two names are said to have read, one carrying franchise history with Vance, the other testing stronger on tone. No offer has gone out, and the part is called on three strips inside the first four shooting days.
